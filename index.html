About (English)

I maintain two small, data-first tools. yiminshuju.com is a Chinese-language site that tracks the U.S. Visa Bulletin (green card priority dates, Final Action and Dates for Filing charts), USCIS processing times and Canada Express Entry draws, always citing the official source. ipforai.cc checks your public egress IP (IPv4/IPv6), ASN, location and proxy/VPN/Tor signals, and tests connectivity to AI services such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.
